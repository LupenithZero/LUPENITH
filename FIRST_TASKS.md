# First Tasks for Contributors

These tasks are intentionally small. They help turn the idea into something other people can examine and improve.

## Charter and governance

- [ ] Identify ambiguous wording in `docs/CHARTER.md` and propose clearer language.
- [ ] Find a scenario in which a leader could still abuse the governance model; propose a safeguard.
- [ ] Review the protected-principles amendment process for logical or practical weaknesses.
- [ ] Suggest fair, accessible election and appeal procedures.
- [ ] Identify ways ranks could accidentally create a social caste and propose prevention measures.

## Evidence and learning

- [ ] Add credible, original sources for claims about scientific literacy, critical thinking, and well-being.
- [ ] Propose a source-review template that separates evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, and values.
- [ ] Suggest an accessible first public guide that can be completed in a few weeks.
- [ ] Review language in the mental-health and self-care sections for overclaims or stigma.

## Open technology

- [ ] Suggest one concrete user problem related to privacy, digital autonomy, accessibility, or open technology.
- [ ] Research existing open-source projects that already address the problem.
- [ ] Compare licenses, maintenance status, threat models, accessibility, and community health.
- [ ] Propose one small upstream contribution rather than starting a new project unnecessarily.

## Community operations

- [ ] Review `CONTRIBUTING.md` for newcomer barriers.
- [ ] Suggest a fair way to credit translators, researchers, reviewers, and technical contributors.
- [ ] Improve the issue templates and project navigation.
- [ ] Suggest a transparent, privacy-preserving way to gather feedback.

## How to claim a task

Open an issue or comment on an existing one. State what you plan to do and the approximate scope. Do not spend significant time on a large task before maintainers clarify acceptance criteria. All work is voluntary unless a separate written paid agreement exists.
