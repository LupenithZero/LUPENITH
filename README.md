# LUPENITH

**Think clearly. Build openly. Stand together.**

**Proposed working name; formal availability checks are still needed.** This is an early-stage, public project exploring how a science-oriented, humanist philosophy can support personal agency, responsible freedom, mutual aid, and open technology.

> **Status:** Draft / seeking criticism and founding collaborators. This is not yet a registered organization, a functioning religion, a charity, a therapy service, a lender, or an established technology platform. No funding, jobs, loans, or services are currently promised.

## Why this project exists

People deserve better tools for understanding the world, making decisions, building useful things, and supporting one another. We want to explore a global community that combines:

- **Evidence over unquestionable authority.** Claims should be open to examination and revised when evidence changes.
- **Personal agency with mutual support.** Build the ability to stand on your own feet, while making it easier to ask for and offer practical help.
- **Freedom with responsibility.** People should be free to live differently, provided they respect other people's rights, consent, safety, and dignity.
- **Open knowledge and technology.** Prefer transparent, auditable, accessible tools and contribute improvements back to the commons.
- **Accountable power.** No founder, elected leader, expert, majority, or AI system is above criticism or due process.
- **Useful results over grand claims.** Judge the project by what it makes better, not by the size of its audience or intensity of anyone's loyalty.

The founder is exploring whether this work could eventually become a global philosophical or religious movement. That future is neither settled nor a condition of participation. **Nobody is being asked to believe, worship, convert, or pledge loyalty.** People can contribute to individual projects without accepting the broader philosophy.

## What we want to build over time

The vision is broad; implementation will be evidence-led and incremental.

1. **A public charter and learning library** about critical thinking, scientific literacy, personal agency, ethics, privacy, and responsible technology.
2. **A global, open collaboration community** for researchers, developers, designers, translators, educators, and people who want to test ideas constructively.
3. **Open-source technology** that improves people's digital autonomy and privacy. Possible long-term areas include privacy tools, browser improvements, video infrastructure, operating systems, accessibility, and security. These are aspirations, not existing products.
4. **Evidence-informed public education** about well-being and mental health, reviewed by suitably qualified professionals where appropriate. This project is not a substitute for professional care.
5. **Practical mutual aid and economic opportunity**, potentially including skills development, transparent job networks, cooperatives, and support funds when lawful structures and sustainable resources exist.
6. **Child and youth support** only through qualified, lawful, independently safeguarded programs, prioritizing children's rights, individual needs, education, and future autonomy.

## What exists today

At this stage, the project is a set of public drafts and a request for thoughtful criticism. There is no product, organization, professional team, dedicated security team, funding pool, or operating service yet. The first objective is to publish a clear charter, create transparent contribution processes, and complete a small useful deliverable with collaborators.

## Foundational commitments

These are proposed protected principles for the project to discuss and test:

- Human dignity and equal basic rights.
- Freedom of conscience, belief, non-belief, criticism, and peaceful disagreement.
- The right to leave a group without threats, harassment, retaliation, or loss of rights outside the group's lawful control.
- No compulsory worship, supernatural belief, personal loyalty, or unquestioning obedience.
- No leader or AI may unilaterally make final punitive decisions.
- No retaliation against good-faith criticism or disclosure of organizational mistakes.
- Privacy by default and collection of only the information genuinely needed.
- Fair process, proportionate responses, evidence review, and a meaningful appeal.
- No discrimination in basic membership rights based on rank, status, wealth, belief, nationality, or personal relationships.
- No aid, employment opportunity, or essential service should be conditional on adopting the project's beliefs.

See [the draft charter](docs/CHARTER.md), [governance model](docs/GOVERNANCE.md), [conduct policy](docs/ETHICS_AND_CONDUCT.md), and [AI governance principles](docs/AI_GOVERNANCE.md).

## How to contribute

You do not need to agree with the whole vision. Critical feedback is welcome, and a small, verifiable contribution is more useful than an enthusiastic promise.

- Review the charter and open a specific issue with suggested wording or a reasoned objection.
- Improve documentation, translation, accessibility, or references.
- Suggest a small technical project with a clear user need and realistic scope.
- Review claims about science or mental health and provide high-quality sources.
- Help design fair, privacy-preserving governance and contributor processes.
- Share relevant open-source projects that the initiative should learn from or contribute to instead of duplicating.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [the first tasks list](FIRST_TASKS.md). Please read the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) before participating.

## What we are not asking for

- We are not asking anyone to treat the founder as an authority.
- We are not recruiting people to cut off family, friends, or outside communities.
- We are not asking for money, donations, personal data, unpaid full-time labor, or pledges of loyalty.
- We are not promising employment, loans, therapy, protection, profits, or a quick solution to life's difficulties.
- We are not claiming to have built a secure browser, video platform, operating system, AI, charity, or global organization.
- We will not use private belief, rank, or criticism of leadership as a reason to deny basic rights.

## Decision-making and open change

The documents in this repository are drafts. Changes should be proposed publicly, discussed respectfully, and recorded with their rationale. Protected rights cannot be removed through ordinary votes. AI may assist analysis but cannot replace accountable human decisions, independent review, or the rights of affected people.

See [GOVERNANCE.md](docs/GOVERNANCE.md) for the proposed amendment model. Until a functioning, independently reviewable process exists, no claimed vote or AI score should be treated as binding authority.

## Financial integrity

The project currently has no operating budget and is not soliciting donations. Long-term economic ideas are described in [ECONOMIC_MODEL.md](docs/ECONOMIC_MODEL.md); they are proposals, not active financial services. Any future business, cooperative, support fund, or social program must have separate accounting, transparent rules, appropriate legal advice, and independent review. No one should transfer money based on this repository.

## Naming and scope

“LUPENITH” is a coined working name, not a legally cleared brand. Before formal or commercial use, check exact-match names, similar spellings, domains, social handles, company registers, and relevant trademark databases in the jurisdictions where the project may operate. Be prepared to change the name if a conflict is found.
