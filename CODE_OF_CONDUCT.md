# Community Code of Conduct

Our goal is a community where people can work together without intimidation, humiliation, or pressure to conform. This code applies to project spaces and to conduct that materially affects participation in them.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and specific, especially when disagreeing.
- Criticize ideas, evidence, and decisions rather than demeaning people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect boundaries, consent, privacy, and different backgrounds.
- Make space for newcomers and contributors with different levels of experience.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Report security and safeguarding concerns responsibly.
- Respect the right to criticize the project, reject its philosophy, or leave.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Discrimination or demeaning treatment based on protected or personal characteristics.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission or a lawful, justified basis.
- Sexual harassment or coercive conduct.
- Fraud, deliberate impersonation, fabricated evidence, or manipulation of votes.
- Retaliation against people who report concerns, challenge leadership, or leave.
- Pressuring people to provide money, unpaid labor, private data, recruitment, worship, or personal loyalty.
- Using rank, expertise, or AI-generated scores to silence dissent.
- Encouraging members to isolate from family, friends, or independent advice.

## Reporting and response

Report concerns through a private message to the repository maintainers on GitHub. Do not include unnecessary sensitive details in public issues. At this early stage, there is no dedicated professional incident-response team, and urgent safety concerns should be directed to appropriate local emergency or support services.

Maintainers should review reports impartially, protect confidentiality as far as practical, consider safety risks, disclose conflicts of interest, and explain outcomes where doing so does not expose affected people. Consequential sanctions should include notice, a chance to respond, proportionality, and an appeal to a different reviewer whenever feasible.

The person handling a complaint must not be the sole decision-maker when they are directly involved in it. Good-faith reports must not trigger retaliation. A report is not proof, and all sides deserve fair process.

## Limits

This code does not authorize the community to replace law enforcement, courts, professional regulators, clinicians, or child-protection services. No internal process guarantees physical safety, confidentiality, or a particular outcome.

## Changes

This code is a draft and may be improved publicly. Changes must preserve the right to dissent, report abuse, and leave freely.
